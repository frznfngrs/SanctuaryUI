format: png
reason: Questie on this Forever client loads Interface textures from PNG via SetTexture (Questie/Icons and MinimapIcon). SanctuaryUI probes each Art slot with SetTexture at login; /sanctuary check prints the count.
upscale: Lanczos to the slot target when the cropped cell's long edge is smaller. Sheets larger than the target are cropped first, not shrunk.

globeLiquid: Base/Textures/2DUI_HealthGlobe_Classic.tex DXT1 2048x512 crop (0, 0, 96, 96) cell 96x96 -> 512x512 globe-liquid.png
globeFrame: BottomBar_GameRes_Classic.tex A8R8G8B8 1416x200 left bezel, glass hole centered, 256 crop -> 512 globe-frame.png
globeFrameResource: same sheet, right bezel -> 512 globe-frame-resource.png. Hole is about 0.53 of the texture, so the globe frame is outset 16 and the liquid inset is 23.
buttonRing: circular metal bezel from the health globe, horn gap closed from the opposite arc, 184 -> 256 button-ring.png and button-ring-small.png

2DUIButtons.tex is DXT5 1024x512 of menu panels, not the skill sockets.
2DUIBottomBar_Classic.tex is DXT3 1024x128 and is not the globe frame.
bottomBar_buttonFX_frameGlow.tex is a square orange glow, not a socket ring.
2DUI_ResourceGlobe_Classic.tex matches the health sheet (2048x512, 96px cells) in blue. The shipped liquid is the health sheet converted to luminance so power color can tint it.
trough, xp, and pip stay on flat-color fallbacks. The console skill sockets are square stone panels, and stretching that strip to the circular row would squash it.
